# Review decisions: PR #165

Answers to the findings of the five review agents on the first head (abfcbb3): R-165-1 to 7; second head (dbb23a2): R-165-8 to 11.

## R-165-1 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The companion keeps the 50 worst findings before Portarr matches KEV, so an exploited high unfixed finding could be cut silently.
- **Where**: scanner/src/report.ts, ExploitIntelligence.cs, ImageVulnerabilityView.cs, ADR 0025
- **Reason**: The companion now returns every vulnerability id of a target (bounded at 5000, `idsTruncated` when cut); Portarr matches KEV and EPSS on all of them, lists the exploited ids beyond the shown findings, raises the danger on them, and says when the list was partial.

## R-165-2 | test | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: ThreatIntelStatus had no test.
- **Where**: frontend/src/Maintenance/__tests__/ThreatIntelStatus.test.tsx
- **Reason**: Three tests: off, read or not yet, error.

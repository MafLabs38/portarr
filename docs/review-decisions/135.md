# Review decisions — PR #135

Answers to the findings of the five review agents: R-135-1 to 29 on the first head (02b7fd1), R-135-30 and following on the second (e0b807e).

## R-135-1 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: Accepting a capture silently drops a workstation whose edit kept conflicting.
- **Where**: McpCaptureService.AddToWorkstationsAsync
- **Reason**: The edit of one workstation is its own method; when every attempt conflicts the workstation is reported in the result's errors.

## R-135-55 | architecture | major | declined

- **Finding**: About 1,000 lines that know specific tools (the nine clients' formats, the vault and az command lines) sit in Portarr.Core, against D-36.
- **Where**: Mcp/Clients, Mcp/Secrets
- **Reason**: They are text generators, not connectors: they never call a tool, hold no credential and need no network, which is what D-36's connector libraries exist to isolate. Moving them to a provider library would add an assembly, a registry and DI plumbing for no isolation gained, and adding a client already touches one class and one list. The decision is recorded in D-113 and ADR 0016. This is the maintainer's to accept.

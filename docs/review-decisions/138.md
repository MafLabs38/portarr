# Review decisions — PR #138

Answers to the findings of the five review agents on the first head (88e7bca): R-138-1 to 30.

## R-138-1 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The size cap of the platform sources never takes effect: MaxResponseContentBufferSize does not limit a streamed read.
- **Where**: Portarr.Providers.Platforms (PlatformsServiceCollectionExtensions, K3sReleaseLineSource, EndOfLifeDateSource)
- **Reason**: The answer is read through a byte cap (2 MB once decompressed) shared by both sources; the unused buffer setting is gone. A test sends an answer over the cap and expects it refused.

## R-138-2 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The GPU exporter's cap is checked after a whole line is read, so a body without a newline is buffered whole.
- **Where**: GpuExporterSource.ParseAsync
- **Reason**: The response stream is wrapped in the capped stream (counting bytes after decompression) before it is read; the character count is removed. CappedStream moves to Core so node_exporter and both new connectors share it; a test sends one line that never ends.

## R-138-3 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: Path.AltDirectorySeparatorChar builds a URL segment.
- **Where**: EndOfLifeDateSource
- **Reason**: SonarQube S1075 refuses a hard-coded delimiter literal here; the constant is the form the analyzer accepts. The product is validated as a plain identifier first, so it cannot reshape the address.

## R-138-4 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: KubernetesVersion keeps the compiler's value equality while CompareTo ignores the text (S1210).
- **Where**: KubernetesVersion
- **Reason**: Equals and GetHashCode now follow CompareTo; a test pins that v1.31.4+k3s1 and 1.31.4+K3S1 are equal.

## R-138-5 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The K3s detection works by accident for a build with no number, and a double blank line.
- **Where**: KubernetesVersion
- **Reason**: The build after the plus sign is parsed generically (letters name the distribution, the trailing number is the build) with tests for k3s, k3s12, K3S2, rke2r1 and a bare number.

## R-138-6 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Where**: Portarr.Providers.Platforms, test projects
- **Reason**: One internal PlatformsHttp helper; StubHttp lives in Portarr.Test.Common.

## R-138-7 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: One slow exporter delays every other card's reading.

## R-138-26 | architecture | major | fixed

## R-138-27 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Reason**: The distribution is read generically from the kubelet version and is the platform key; Core asks for the lines of whatever distribution the nodes name and the source that carries the name answers; the source gives its display name. ## R-138-29 | architecture | minor | fixed

## R-138-30 | architecture | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The GPU interval is fixed while host metrics allow a per-machine interval.
- **Where**: GpuMonitor
- **Reason**: Stated in D-120 and the connector page: the interval is deliberately fixed at 5 seconds until someone needs another; the hostMetrics interval exists because that exporter is read every second.

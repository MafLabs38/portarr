Answers to the findings of the five review agents on the first head (88e7bca): R-138-1 to 30 on the first head (88e7bca), R-138-31 to 48 on the second (0365ca1), R-138-49 to 60 on the third (0eb3e17), R-138-61 to 75 on the fourth (4d40d4d), R-138-76 to 87 on the fifth (fef8158), R-138-88 to 93 on the sixth (0eaee90), R-138-94 and following on the seventh (020c1a5).

## R-138-1 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The size cap of the platform sources never takes effect: MaxResponseContentBufferSize does not limit a streamed read.
- **Where**: Portarr.Providers.Platforms (PlatformsServiceCollectionExtensions, K3sReleaseLineSource, EndOfLifeDateSource)
- **Reason**: The answer is read through a byte cap (2 MB once decompressed) shared by both sources; the unused buffer setting is gone. A test sends an answer over the cap and expects it refused.

## R-138-2 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The GPU exporter's cap is checked after a whole line is read, so a body without a newline is buffered whole.
- **Where**: GpuExporterSource.ParseAsync
- **Reason**: The response stream is wrapped in the capped stream (counting bytes after decompression) before it is read; the character count is removed. CappedStream moves to Core so node_exporter and both new connectors share it; a test sends one line that never ends.

## R-138-3 | quality | minor | declined

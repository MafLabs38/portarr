# 0003 — GPL-3.0 license

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-28
- **Spec**: D-27

## Context

Sonarr and Radarr are GPL-3.0. Portarr takes their patterns and may reuse some of their components.

## Decision

Portarr is licensed under the **GNU GPL v3.0 or later** (`LICENSE`).

## Consequences

- Code adapted from Sonarr/Radarr is allowed, with attribution in the file header.
- Derivative works of Portarr must stay open source under the GPL.
